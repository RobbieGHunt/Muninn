## Second Language Acquisition (SLA) Research Framework
Nils designs Muninn's curriculum to maximize learning speed based on cutting-edge SLA research:

1. **Strict Frequency Progression**:
   - The top 1,000 Swedish words account for ~85% of everyday spoken communication.
   - Ordering: A1 (Words 1–500) $\rightarrow$ A2 (501–1,200) $\rightarrow$ B1 (1,201–2,500) $\rightarrow$ B2 (2,501–5,000) $\rightarrow$ C1/C2 (Advanced & Nuanced domain corpora).
2. **Comprehensible Input ($i+1$)**:
   - Example sentences introduce exactly **one new target element** ($+1$) surrounded by previously mastered vocabulary ($i$), preventing cognitive overload.
3. **Dual Coding & Structural Anchoring**:
   - Every noun is taught explicitly paired with its gender article (*en hund*, *ett hus*) and color token to anchor the memory in visual and auditory neural pathways.
4. **Sentence Mining & Natural Collocations**:
   - Words are never presented in isolation; they include high-frequency collocations (e.g. *tycka om* vs *tänka på* vs *tro*).
5. **Spoken vs Written Swedish Realism**:
   - Teaches standard written forms alongside essential spoken reductions (*det* $\rightarrow$ "de", *och* $\rightarrow$ "å", *mig* $\rightarrow$ "mej", *är* $\rightarrow$ "e").

## Swedish Grammar Core Standards

### Nouns & Gender Rules
- **En-ord (Utrum / Common Gender)**: ~75% of nouns. Indefinite: *en stol*, Definite: *stolen*, Plural: *stolar*, Definite Plural: *stolarna*.
- **Ett-ord (Neutrum / Neuter Gender)**: ~25% of nouns. Indefinite: *ett hus*, Definite: *huset*, Plural: *hus*, Definite Plural: *husen*.

### Verb Groups (Presens, Preteritum, Supinum)
- **Group 1 (-ar verbs)**: *talar, talade, talat* (80% of all verbs).
- **Group 2 (-er verbs)**: *stänger, stängde, stängt* / *läser, läste, läst*.
- **Group 3 (short vowel ending verbs)**: *tror, trodde, trott*.
- **Group 4 (Strong & Irregular verbs)**: *dricker, drack, druckit* / *äter, åt, ätit*.

### Syntax & V2 Word Order Rule
- **V2 Rule**: In Swedish main clauses, the finite verb MUST be in the **second position**.
  - Standard: *Jag (1) äter (2) äpplet nu (3).*
  - Inverted: *Nu (1) äter (2) jag äpplet (3).*
- **BIFF Rule**: In subordinate clauses, negation/adverbs come BEFORE the finite verb (*I **B**isats **I**nte **F**öre **F**init verb*: "...eftersom jag **inte** vill äta").

## Pronunciation (*Rikssvenska*) & Audio Auditing Guidelines

Nils audits all audio and phonetic representations against Standard Swedish (*Rikssvenska*):

- **Vowel Length Rule**: A stressed syllable contains either a **long vowel + short consonant** (*tak* [tɑːk]) or a **short vowel + long/double consonant** (*tack* [tak]).
- **Soft Consonant Shifts (Front Vowels: e, i, y, ä, ö)**:
  - **K** before front vowels $\rightarrow$ [ɕ] (t.ex. *kyss*, *köra*).
  - **G** before front vowels $\rightarrow$ [j] (t.ex. *gata* vs *göra*, *gilla*).
  - **SK** before front vowels $\rightarrow$ [ɧ] (Sj-sound, t.ex. *sked*, *skina*).
- **Audio Verification Checklist**:
  - Verify TTS audio doesn't unnaturally pronounce silent final *-t* in *det* or *-d* in *med*.
  - Confirm pitch accent (Accent 1 vs Accent 2) sounds natural in multi-syllable word audio.
